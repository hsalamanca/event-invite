Ownvite logo assets
===================

Files
-----
  ownvite-logo.svg   Horizontal wordmark + gold spark (site header)
  ownvite-mark.svg   Square spark mark only (favicon / app icon)

Colors
------
  Ink navy       #0F1A2E  — wordmark
  Champagne gold #C9A962  — accent spark only
  Background     transparent

Usage
-----
  Light backgrounds: use as-is (navy wordmark, gold spark).
  Dark backgrounds: recolor the wordmark paths to white (#FFFFFF);
                    keep the gold spark (#C9A962) unchanged.
                    Do not recolor the spark to white or navy.

Minimum sizes
-------------
  Wordmark (ownvite-logo.svg):  120px wide (~40px tall at native ratio) minimum
  Mark (ownvite-mark.svg):      16×16 px minimum (favicon); prefer 32×32+

Notes
-----
  Flat vector only — no gradients, glow, or purple.
  Wordmark is outlined serif letterforms (self-contained paths).
  Leave clear space roughly equal to the spark’s height around the mark.
